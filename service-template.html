Case study 02 · Template translation

Timed English SRT into pt-BR, inside every CPS and CPL limit.

Netflix SPT, Amazon, and generic formats — kept consistent across seasons and episodes, with the reading speed the brief actually sets.

EN → PT-BRNetflix SPTAmazonSRT / VTTCPS / CPL

A template is a promise to the next episode.

Every platform has its own template: line count, reading speed, character limit per line, on-screen text handling. The same episode translated into two different templates reads like two different shows.

This case is the same cue, rendered in three templates — Netflix SPT, Amazon, and a generic SRT — to show what changes and what doesn't.

What changes between templates.

Line count changes what can be said on one line. A line that fits Amazon breaks on Netflix.

Reading speed is the same in most streaming templates — but legacy broadcast templates often allow 20 CPS, and older DVD templates 22.

On-screen text is handled differently: hard-coded, forced narrative, or translated only when it carries meaning the dialogue doesn't.

The translation stays the same. The way it is shown on screen does not.
