What the QC pass shows.

Timing against the picture. A cue that starts before or ends after a shot change is caught on the timeline, not just in the text.

Reading speed inside the brief. The CPS limit is the one the delivery asks for — never a universal number.

Terminology checked, not guessed. If a term cannot be confirmed against a brief or glossary, the reviewer marks it for follow-up rather than inventing a “fix”.

On-screen text accounted for. Hard-coded text is either translated or represented as a forced narrative, depending on what the brief allows.
