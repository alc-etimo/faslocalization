Case study 04 · QC & delivery

The last mile is technical. Catch it before the client does.

A subtitle file can be perfectly translated and still fail delivery. Timing that crosses a shot change. Reading speed that ignores the brief. Spelling that slips past the eye. QC is the checklist that turns a draft into a deliverable.

QC is a checklist, not a gut feeling.

Most subtitle rejections are not translation errors. They are technical: a cue that starts 2 frames before a cut, a CPS that goes over the Netflix limit, a double space. A good QC pass runs the checks systematically, resolves them, and generates a report the PM can attach to the delivery.

The mock below simulates that workflow. Run the QC to see the issues populate. Switch tabs to see the category. Fix the timing issue by dragging the cue. Generate the report when everything is resolved.
