What the pass checks.

Timing. Cue starts and ends against shot changes, not just against each other.

Reading speed. CPS within the brief, not the same as the previous episode.

Spelling. Names, places, terminology — the things the spellchecker will not catch.

On-screen text. Forced narratives, hard-coded titles, anything a viewer reads and the subtitles miss.

The file only ships after the pass. Not before.
