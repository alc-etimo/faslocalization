"lol" → "kkk", "brb" → "já venho", "idk" → "sei lá", "omg" → "nossa".
The translation preserves social function and screen economy, not literal words.

The workflow.

01

Contextual Analysis

Read the scene, identify character relationships, narrative intent, register, cultural references, and audiovisual context.

02

Dialogue Localization

Adapt dialogue into natural Brazilian Portuguese, preserving meaning, tone, character voice, humor, and idiomatic intent.

03

Subtitle Adaptation

Fit localized dialogue to timing, reading speed, segmentation, line length, and visual context.

04

Final Review

Check consistency, spelling, punctuation, readability, timing, and on-screen text against the audiovisual material.

Linguistic quality and technical subtitle quality are inseparable. A line that works on the page but not on the screen is not done.
