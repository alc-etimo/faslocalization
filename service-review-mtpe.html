What a PM needs to see.

When you deliver an MTPE pass, the client doesn't just want the corrected text — they want to know what you changed and why. A good edit log turns an opaque rewrite into a transparent list of decisions: category, severity, and the exact before/after.

The MTPE workflow.

01

Read for meaning, then for register

Machine translation often misses the tone. "Fosse embora" is grammatically correct, but "fosse embora" in a casual scene feels wrong. The edit is a register fix, not a grammar fix.

02

Classify the error

A severity tag changes what the PM does next. Critical errors block delivery. Major errors need review. Minor errors are polish. The classification is the review.

03

Log the fix, not just the change

The log should say "from X to Y · Register · Major" so the PM doesn't have to diff the text. The button is the audit trail.
